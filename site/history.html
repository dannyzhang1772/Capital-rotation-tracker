Thirty years of momentum vs defensive, inside the sectors

Capital does not only move between industries; within each one it rotates between the high-momentum, high-beta names and the steady, low-volatility names (NVDA versus the quality names in semis, the cruise lines versus the hotels in leisure). Every month each sub-industry's members are sorted by style score (12-1 month momentum minus defensiveness, where defensiveness is low volatility and low beta); the top third is the momentum cohort, the bottom third the defensive cohort. Both are cap-weighted, held a month and chain-linked. The spread is momentum divided by defensive.

Backdrop: Fama-French daily factors, 1995 to today

Kenneth French's academic long-short factors, cumulative, rebased to 100 (log scale). Momentum (winners minus losers) and quality (robust minus weak profitability) are the external check on the cohort series; value and size for context.

Where each sub-industry's style spread stands now

Spread = momentum cohort return minus defensive cohort return, compounded. z (10y) = where the log spread sits against its own trailing ten-year mean and standard deviation. Mom. cap share = the momentum cohort's share of the two cohorts' combined market cap. HF tilt = share of the curated hedge-fund group's 13F value in the bucket that sits in the momentum cohort.
