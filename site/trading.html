Trading overview

Load a portfolio

One holding per line: TICKER, quantity, average cost, then optionally currency (CAD or USD) and an account name. Example: SHOP, 20, 90.50, CAD, TFSA. Use the ticker as your broker shows it; Canadian listings are found automatically for CAD lines.

Brokerage links go through SnapTrade with read-only access; this site never sees your brokerage password and cannot place trades. Holdings are sent to the projectmodl analysis service, analysed and returned to this browser. They are not stored on the server or published, and they disappear from this browser when the tab closes.

Returns decomposition: what drove the return

Each holding's daily return is regressed on the market, its sector, five style factors and the currency over the trailing year; the fitted pieces are then added up over the window you pick. What the factors cannot explain is idiosyncratic: the stock's own news. Numbers are percentage points of return in CAD.

Which holdings did it (contribution to the book's return)

Each bar is one holding's weight times its return, split by driver.

Market = S&P 500 for US-listed names, the TSX 60 for Canadian ones. Sector = the sector ETF minus the S&P 500. Style = growth minus value, small minus large, momentum, quality and low-vol ETF spreads. R² is how much of the daily variance the factors explain; 1 − R² is the idiosyncratic share of risk.

Expected moves from the options market

For names with listed options, the at-the-money straddle prices the move the market expects by expiry, and the implied volatility gives a one-month one-standard-deviation range. Where an expiry sits just after an earnings date, the extra variance in that expiry is the implied earnings move. TSX-listed names have no chains on the free feed, so they fall back to three-month realised volatility.

Scenarios push one factor and pass it through each holding's fitted beta; unhedged US-dollar positions take currency moves one for one. "On equity" scales by leverage, because the margin loan does not shrink when prices fall. These are sensitivities, not forecasts, and betas drift.

How to read this page

Positions read from the brokerage on the date shown; prices and option chains from Yahoo Finance (delayed). Exposures and scenarios are descriptive analytics, not investment advice.
