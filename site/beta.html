Beta Mode: Opportunities

Every researched event is rated for how material it was to the sub-industry's fundamentals on a blind rubric (-100 to +100), then compared with what capital actually did afterwards. The gap between the two is the mispricing candidate. The backtest below reports, without editing, whether such gaps have historically closed. Research project, not advice.
